Upcorn — a fluffy unicorn slingshot climber

Hold, drag down to aim, release to launch a fluffy unicorn up an endless, ever-morphing tower and don't look down.

Mobile-first, one-finger arcade endlessly-scrolling climber.

---

In upcorn you're a tiny unicorn at the bottom of an impossible tower. A quick press of the screen starts a slingshot. Drag down to dial in your launch angle and power, release and watch it soar. Land on the next platform, sling again, repeat. The sky changes as you climb: meadows give way to cloud layer, stormy wind, deep space with nebulae, a moon, planets and slowly ringed worlds. Every 100m is a milestone; every launch seeks a rainbow-perfect triple jump.

Controls (mobile-first)

     - Tap → start pull
     - Drag down → aim (drag lower = stronger)
     - Release → launch
     - Fully playable with one finger.
